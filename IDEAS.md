# Triangulate: ideas and roadmap

## 1. Make the money engine work
1. **Self-serve venue portal.** Venues claim their OSM listing, upload photos and an offer, and pay by card (Stripe Checkout). Price per "zone impression" (shown to a group whose meeting zone contains them) rather than per click. It's a sharper pitch than Google Ads: "we only show you to groups who are literally choosing somewhere near you."
2. **Group-size targeting.** Sponsors bid more for groups of 6+ (bigger spend). Show "Great for groups of 8" badges.
3. **Real booking integrations.** Affiliate or partner APIs (OpenTable, ResDiary, SevenRooms, DesignMyNight, Fever for experiences, Eventbrite for gigs) earn per-booking commission, and booking happens in the app.
4. **Google Ads landing pages.** Static SEO pages such as "Halfway between Camden and Peckham" or "Fair place to meet in Melbourne CBD". Generated by city pair, they're perfect long-tail ad and organic landing pages. Each deep-links into the app pre-filled.
5. **Conversion tracking.** GA4 events are already wired. Import `book_click` as the Ads conversion and use the sponsored flag to prove ROI to venues.
6. **"Triangulate Picks" as a curated tier.** An editorial best-of per area builds trust in the list, so sponsored slots don't feel like the whole list is bought.

## 2. Features people will love
1. **Public transport mode.** The #1 ask in cities. Needs a transit router (OpenTripPlanner self-hosted, or the TravelTime / Google Routes API).
2. **Group voting.** Share a link where friends drop their own pin (no typing addresses for others) and vote 👍 on 3 shortlisted venues. The winning venue gets a "Book it" button. This also drives virality, since every user invites 2–9 more.
3. **Calendar availability.** "When are we all free?" via Google/Outlook free-busy, combined with opening hours: "Thursday 7pm, The Crown, everyone ≤ 20 min."
4. **Mixed travel modes.** One friend drives, one cycles, one takes the train. Score each person on their own mode.
5. **Time-of-day aware.** Rush-hour traffic, last train home, venues open at the planned time (opening_hours parsing).
6. **Weighting and constraints.** "Sam has a baby, keep their trip shortest", "Avoid the CBD", "Must have parking", "Step-free".
7. **Isochrone view.** Shade where everyone can reach within X minutes; the overlap is the fair zone.
8. **Venue vibes.** Filters like quiet enough to talk, kid-friendly, dog-friendly, beer garden, late-night, budget £/££/£££, vegan.
9. **Weather-aware suggestions.** Sunny means parks and beer gardens; rain means indoor games and cinemas.
10. **Recurring meet-ups.** Save a group ("Uni mates") and rotate the fair spot each month so nobody hosts twice.
11. **Cost fairness.** Factor in fuel, tolls, parking and transit fares: equal cost as well as equal time.
12. **"Meet on the way."** For two people heading somewhere later, find a spot along both routes.

## 3. Growth
- WhatsApp/iMessage-first flow: a bot ("@Triangulate where should we meet?") that collects pins in-chat.
- Pretty share cards (OG image of the map with the venue) so links look great in group chats.
- PWA install and offline-saved plans.
- Partnerships with dating apps (first-date halfway points) and sports clubs (fair training venues).

## 4. Tech upgrades when traffic grows
- Swap Overpass for a fast places API (Foursquare Places has a free tier; Google Places for rich photos and ratings) behind a tiny serverless proxy (Cloudflare Workers, free tier) that also caches.
- Self-host OSRM/Valhalla (or use a paid matrix API) to avoid public demo-server limits.
- Custom domain (triangulate.app or similar) for Ads quality score and trust.
